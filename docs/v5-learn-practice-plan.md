# Mouna v5 plan: Learn, Practice and stories

Status: idea agreed on 2026-11-24, not started. Build it as v5 (new branch from v4),
prototype the story generator in the lab first.

## The idea
- **Learn tab**: today's per-sign coach (watch, prep checks, record, Score tab).
- **Dashboard**: what the learner has learned, and how solid each sign is.
- **Practice tab**: only learned signs. First quick single-sign review (spaced
  repetition), then short stories made of learned signs, generated by the LLM as needed.

## Stories: the two hard parts
1. **ASL is not English word-for-word.** Stories must be ASL gloss order
   (e.g. YESTERDAY ME SCHOOL GO), not English. LLMs are shaky at ASL grammar, so:
   - give the model only the learner's learned signs plus a few simple sentence patterns;
   - reject in code any story using a sign the learner hasn't learned;
   - show the English meaning next to the glosses;
   - have a fluent signer check the sentence patterns once.
   Facial grammar (e.g. raised brows for questions) stays unscored.
2. **Grading only knows isolated signs.** Stage it:
   - *Guided*: highlight each sign of the story in turn; record and grade one at a time
     (reuses all of v4).
   - *Whole sentence*: sign the full line; split it using the known sign order (forced
     alignment, far easier than open recognition). Coarticulation makes this harder, so
     benchmark first (How2Sign has continuous ASL; check its licence).
   - Gemini: one call per new story, cached and reused.

## Vocabulary to add (~40 story signs)
Our model's 100 signs have no pronouns and few time words. ASL-LEX / ASL Citizen already
has: me, you, he, we, they, my, your, where, when, why, today, tomorrow, yesterday, home,
father, friend, name, happy, tired, work, play. Cost: review time (~8 approvals each,
~320 clips). They grade untrained at first (held-out: 79% of correct clips "good" vs 98%
for trained signs); retrain once the batch is in, then rebuild references and recalibrate.
Pronouns are pointing (index handshape + direction): our vectors already measure both.

## Alphabet (separate model)
- Single letters: 24 of 26 are held handshapes (J, Z move). The joint-angle cloud check
  fits; ~8 examples per letter (record our own, or MediaPipe's custom gesture recogniser
  on a licence-checked alphabet image set).
- Spelled words (names): Google FSboard, 3M+ characters of fingerspelling as MediaPipe
  landmarks, CC BY 4.0 (arXiv 2407.15806).
- More vocabulary: PopSign ASL v1.0, 250 signs, CC BY 4.0 (use the original release;
  the Kaggle copies' licence wasn't confirmed).

## Order
1. Learn / Practice split, with single-sign review.
2. ~40 story signs from ASL Citizen, then retrain.
3. LLM stories (checked by code), guided sign-by-sign practice.
4. Whole-sentence grading, only after it passes the benchmark.
5. Alphabet: single letters, then spelled names ("MY NAME ___").
